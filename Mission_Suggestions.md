# Project 180 — Mission Suggestion Library

**Working draft v0.1** | November 20, 2026 | Drafted by Claude, needs founder review

These are the suggested first promises shown in onboarding (Wireframes §11, O3) and in **Add a mission**. Users can always edit the wording, amount, and schedule, or write their own.

---

## 1. Writing rules

Every suggestion must pass all of these:

1. **Small enough to keep on a hard day.** Prefer 5–10 minutes or a single action.
2. **Add, don't restrict.** Suggest doing something positive ("vegetable with dinner") rather than cutting something out ("no sugar"). No calorie counts, fasting, or elimination.
3. **Clearly done or not done.** The user should know without thinking whether they kept it.
4. **No health claims.** Never say what a promise will *do* for the body ("burns fat," "boosts metabolism").
5. **Not medical advice.** Medication suggestions only organize what a clinician already prescribed. No doses and no supplements beyond the user's own vitamins.
6. **Plain, adult, first-person wording.** Short phrases, no exclamation marks, no emoji, no fitness slang.
7. **Original wording.** Not copied from books, programs, or other apps.

## 2. Review process

**Proposed:**

1. Claude drafts (this file).
2. The founder reviews every line for voice and fit.
3. Before public launch, a qualified health professional (e.g., a physician, registered dietitian, or physical therapist) reviews the Movement, Strength, Better eating, Healthier weight, and Medication items for safety for adults aged 35–70.
4. Changes are logged in §6 with a date.

## 3. Format

Each suggestion has a default schedule and time of day. These pre-fill O4 so the user can just press Continue.

- **Days:** Daily, Weekdays, or specific days (e.g., Mon/Wed/Fri)
- **Time:** Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Anytime
- **Adjustable:** the part the user is invited to change (shown as an editable field)

## 5. Open items

2. Whether suggestions should differ by experience stage later (e.g., offering a 20-minute walk once a 10-minute walk is well established). This must be framed as an invitation, never as pressure (plan §10).
3. Localization (units, meal norms, and local wording).
